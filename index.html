About Us

The Research Group of AI for Industrial Digital Servitization (RAIDS) was established in 2020 at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University.

Vision

Towards NEXT-Generation Human-Machine Symbiotic Manufacturing Systems, Equipment & Scenarios

Value

SURE: Solid, Unique, Radical, Elite.

Mission

To develop advanced robotic solutions, digitalized services, and AI agents for more natural human-machine interaction, efficient multi-robot/human-robot collaboration, and industrial embodied intelligence implementation.
