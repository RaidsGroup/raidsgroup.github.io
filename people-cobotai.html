RAIDS CobotAI

About Us

Towards Next-Generation Industrial Embodied Intelligence —
Multimodal Data Acquisition Terminals and an Embodied Skill-Learning Platform

CobotAI Limited, an HKSTP incubatee spun out of RAIDS at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, focuses on solving the “last-mile” challenge of industrial embodied intelligence: turning multimodal robot data, human expertise, and skill-learning algorithms into practical systems for real manufacturing scenarios. Its core platforms include the Tele𝓧/Ego𝓧 series of multimodal data acquisition terminals and PhysLab, a robotic skill-learning platform for accelerating data collection, policy learning, and deployment. Through partnerships with leading manufacturing, robotics, and AI companies, CobotAI contributes to an emerging ecosystem aligned with the EU’s Industry 5.0 vision, China’s 15th Five-Year Plan and “AI+” initiative, and next-generation smart manufacturing.
